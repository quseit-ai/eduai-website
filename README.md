# EduMate AI Assistant — Release Notes

## A Message to Teachers

Thank you for choosing the Teacher Teaching AI Assistant. We are a development team focused on educational informatization, and we know that lesson preparation, grading, learning analytics, and parent communication take up a great deal of teachers' time. That is why we built this AI assistant — purpose-built for teaching work. Through the combination of **natural-language conversation + a Skills Marketplace + data connectivity**, we hope to help you hand off routine and repetitive tasks to the assistant, so that time can be given back to teaching itself.

This document is written for end users and introduces the features we provide for you, how to use them, and what to keep in mind.

## I. What We Can Do for You

### 1. Lesson Preparation — From Zero to a Usable Lesson Plan in One Sentence

- **One-click lesson plan generation**: Simply tell us the **teaching level + topic + class length**, and we will generate a complete lesson design, including objectives, success criteria, classroom introduction, instruction, practice, checks for understanding, and an exit ticket — with differentiation built in.
- **Tiered teaching materials**: For the same lesson, we automatically generate three tiered versions — **Foundation / Standard / Challenge** — so you can address different levels of students with ease.
- **Dynamic courseware creation**: Generate interactive HTML courseware grounded in a knowledge graph, supporting classroom interactions and text-to-speech playback, with an embedded knowledge graph that makes key concepts clear at a glance.
- **Lesson design refinement**: Apply the ADDIE instructional-design framework to systematically review and iteratively improve your lesson design, helping you polish every detail.

### 2. Homework & Assessment — Tiered, Gradable, and Traceable

- **Tiered homework design**: Generate homework with a **Foundation 60% / Standard 30% / Challenge 10%** split, with recommended time budgeted for each item and a teacher answer key automatically included. You can also share error data from the previous round of grading, and we will use it to design a more targeted homework set next time.
- **Practice exams & exit tickets**: Quickly generate test papers from topics or syllabi, mixing item types and difficulty levels, with a complete answer key and explanations.
- **Rubric builder**: Create clear, gradable rubrics for assignments, projects, presentations, or writing tasks with one click.
- **Essay feedback**: Provide rubric-based, structured feedback on student writing — guiding without rewriting — to protect students' independent thinking.

### 3. Learning Analytics — Beyond the Average Score, to Where the Real Problems Are

- **Class test / error-pattern analysis**: Hand us your score distribution and high-frequency error items, and we will perform **C/P/R/Q error attribution** (Conceptual / Procedural / Careless / Question-reading), prioritize remediation based on **prerequisite + error rate + mark weight**, and deliver an actionable re-teaching plan with measurable verification indicators — you will see whether it worked in the next lesson's exit ticket.

### 4. Caring for Every Student — Tutoring Resources On Demand

- **Socratic tutoring**: Use guiding questions to help students "figure it out" themselves rather than handing over answers.
- **Plain-language concept explanations**: Make hard concepts click through everyday analogies, and instantly check whether students really understand.
- **Flashcards**: Convert notes or chapters into spaced-repetition flashcards with one click (Anki import supported).
- **Pre-exam anxiety support**: Provide anxious students with grounding, triage, and a short action plan.
- **Speech rehearsal, Python onboarding, peer-conflict roleplay**, and more — covering a wide range of student growth scenarios.

### 5. Parent Communication — Professional, Warm, and Time-Saving

- **Parent feedback reports**: Generate parent-meeting feedback and everyday home-school messages with one click — professionally worded, specific, and warm — so parents can see their child's progress.

### 6. Documents & Daily Office Work

- **Document recognition**: Scans, photographs, and handwritten documents can all be recognized and converted to text; PDFs support direct editing (fix typos, change titles).
- **Office processing**: Generate, edit, and batch-process Word / Excel / PowerPoint; auto-fill tables and forms.
- **Slide decks**: Turn outlines into ready-to-use presentations with one click.
- **Email & calendar**: Send and receive email, manage your calendar, and auto-generate meeting minutes.
- **Note management**: Connect to your note library (e.g., Obsidian) to help you search, organize, and preserve teaching materials.

### 7. Teaching Materials — Graphics, Animations, Audio & Video

- **Infographics / posters**: Multiple layouts and styles, ideal for classroom display and event promotion.
- **Math / algorithm animations**: 3Blue1Brown-style animations, perfect for explaining abstract concepts.
- **Courseware prototypes, AI music, ASCII videos**, and other creative assets to make your classroom more engaging.

### 8. Data Connectivity & Automation (Available Within Your School's Authorization)

- **Enterprise knowledge base**: Search and save your school's internal materials (curriculum guides, rules and regulations, etc.).
- **Business databases**: Query and analyze school business data according to your permissions.
- **WeCom documents**: Search, read, and append to your school's WeCom documents.
- **Scheduled tasks**: Set up recurring tasks (e.g., daily learning-analytics briefings, document monitoring, automatic summaries), and have results automatically delivered to your designated channels — no need to stay glued to your computer.

## II. Skills Marketplace — Our Continuous Updates, Your Plug-and-Play

We have built in a **Skills Marketplace** that keeps adding new skills. **22 skills** are available now, with education as the focus. Install and use instantly — no programming required:

- **Lesson planning**: lesson-plan design, tiered teaching, differentiated teaching, ADDIE design, dynamic courseware creation;
- **Homework & assessment**: class learning analytics, homework design, practice-exam generation, rubric builder, essay feedback;
- **Student tutoring**: concept explanation, Socratic tutoring, flashcards, speech rehearsal, exam-anxiety support, Python onboarding, peer-conflict coaching;
- **Office**: document OCR, Office form auto-fill;
- **General**: skill generator.

**Spotlight — the Skill Generator**: If you want to turn your school's own homework templates, workflows, and teaching SOPs into a dedicated skill, just hand us the process and samples, and we will generate a **school-exclusive skill** with one click. The entire school can reuse it, building digital assets that belong to you.

## III. How to Use It

- Just type your needs in the chat box in natural language, for example:
  - "Design a P4 lesson plan on fractions."
  - "Design a tiered homework set based on the error patterns from last week's quiz."
  - "Convert this scanned test paper into an editable Word document."
- Install skills from the Marketplace as needed; they take effect immediately after installation.
- Features involving school data (knowledge base, databases, WeCom) are subject to the permissions granted to you by your administrator.
